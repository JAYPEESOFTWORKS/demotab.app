What it does

Paste anything. Display beautifully.

DemoTab is a single browser tab you load before a screen share or presentation. Paste your content — code, markdown notes, a screenshot, a block of text — and it renders cleanly for your audience without any of the distractions of your usual editor.

Privacy

We genuinely cannot see what you paste.

This isn't just a promise — it's enforced by your browser. DemoTab ships with a Content Security Policy header that makes external network requests technically impossible:

Content-Security-Policy

connect-src 'none' — The browser will refuse any attempt by the app to send data anywhere. You can verify this yourself by opening DevTools on the DemoTab tab and checking the response headers. No code we could ever write would override it.

All rendering happens locally using your browser's built-in capabilities. There are no servers receiving your content, no logs, no databases.

How to use it

Three steps.

1. Open demotab.app in a tab before your screen share or presentation.

2. Share that tab (or your whole screen) with your audience.

3. Paste or drop whatever you want to show. It renders instantly. Press Esc or click Clear to reset.
